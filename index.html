Job Search

Job Search is a small private tool that helps one person find job postings and keep track of applications.

It reads public job listings and job alert emails, scores how well each posting fits, and places the results in a Google Sheet and Google Docs that the tool itself creates. It does not apply to jobs, post anything, or send email to anyone other than its owner.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
